Four generations of your direct line, branching back to the 1500s on the Yorkshire Renton side, to 1830s Molise on the Italian side, and — newly opened by Geoff's charts — to the 1600s–1700s across your mother's Day family. Deep roots on your mother's side — the Day lines

From Geoff's charts. Both of Betty's parents were Days, from two separate families.

Geoff's research opens up the whole maternal Day side, which was blank until now. It runs back through the wool towns of the West Riding on Frederick's side, and through Somerset, the Isle of Wight and Northamptonshire on Doris's. Read each list top-down: indented names are the parents of the name above. Direct surname lines are inked in green; “solo” names are where only one parent was recorded.

Doris Catherine Day's line · Crewkerne, the Isle of Wight & Northamptonshire
